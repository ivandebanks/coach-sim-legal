Report a game issue

Include your device model, operating system, game version, and the steps that led to the issue. If relevant, tell us your school or state, and whether the issue happened before or during a game. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or Pocket Pass codes.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases in the game with the same store account that made the purchase. To manage or cancel a subscription, open the subscription settings for your Apple or Google account.

Privacy requests

Use the same support address for privacy or data deletion requests and include the game name.
