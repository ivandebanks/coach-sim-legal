High School Basketball Coach Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026 · Applies to the basketball game.

Data on your device

The game stores your saves, roster, career progress, settings, currency and gameplay counters on your device. You can remove local data using the reset controls in Settings or by deleting the app.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple or Google handles payments. The game does not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat processes an app user ID and purchase or receipt information to validate purchases and manage or restore access.

Basketball Pocket Pass

When the game requests, redeems, or rechecks a Pocket Pass, our service hosted by Supabase receives the RevenueCat app user ID, game identifier, and pass code when applicable. It uses that information to verify eligibility and provide access while the qualifying subscription is active. Share a pass code only with someone you intend to give access to it.

Career transfer

You can choose to create and copy a career transfer link to continue an eligible career in College Basketball Coach Sim. A transfer link can contain the in-game name, role, and selected career history. The link carries that information itself; the transfer does not upload your full save to a transfer service. Share links only with people or devices you trust.

Optional Apple sign-in

If you choose Sign in with Apple, the game stores your app-specific Apple user ID and the display name you choose to share. You can sign out in Settings. You can play without signing in.

Approximate region

The game requests a state-level estimate from GeoJS, with ipapi.co as a fallback, to put your likely home state first in the state picker. These providers receive your IP address when the game requests the estimate. The game caches the state result on your device for up to one week. It does not request GPS access.

Advertising

We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-company advertising tracking.

Support, retention, and deletion

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you include. We use that information to respond and keep it as needed to handle your request. Contact snapit.foranything@gmail.com to ask about access to or deletion of data we hold, including information related to Pocket Pass. Deleting the app does not cancel store subscriptions; manage those through your Apple or Google account.
