Common questions

How do I restore a purchase? Use Restore Purchases from the subscription screen. Purchases restore to the same App Store or Google Play account they were bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription? Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, not inside the App.

How do I erase my data? Use the in-app reset control, or delete and reinstall the App.
