1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pro Football Coach handles information in the mobile game.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Save files, rosters, careers, settings, in-game currency balances and gameplay/progress counters are stored locally on your device. Deleting the App removes local game data.

The App has no analytics provider attached and sends no gameplay analytics events off your device. If that changes, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that build ships.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers auto-renewable subscriptions, one-time unlocks and consumable coin packs. Payments are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases are managed through RevenueCat, which validates receipts and manages entitlements; RevenueCat receives an app user ID and purchase/receipt information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Career Transfer

Career Transfer creates a self-contained link from the career snapshot you explicitly choose to transfer. The link can contain player or coach identity, career history and related transfer data. It is staged locally by the destination game and does not require a transfer server or automatically upload your full save file.

Transfer data leaves your device only when you choose to share or open that link. Treat a Career Transfer link like a save export and share it only with people or devices you trust.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

No camera, microphone, or photo library access. No precise location data. No advertising SDKs, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking. No sale of personal data.

6. Device Permissions

The App uses the Android VIBRATE permission solely for haptic feedback.

7. Deleting Your Data

Local game data can be removed with the in-app reset control or by deleting the App. Any staged Career Transfer data is local app data and is removed with the rest of the App data.

8. Third-Party Services

RevenueCat (in-app purchase and subscription management); Apple App Store / Google Play (payment processing and app distribution). No advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs are integrated.

9. Children's Privacy

Pro Football Coach is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact snapit.foranything@gmail.com if you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App.

10. Your Rights

EEA residents have GDPR rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. California residents have applicable CCPA rights. Contact us at the email below to exercise any applicable right.

11. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes by updating the Effective Date above.
