Twichie Support

For help, email snapit.foranything@gmail.com.

Report an issue

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, the Twichie version, and the steps that led to the issue. Tell us which test you were running (reflex, punch speed, or Power Score). Please do not include passwords or payment details.

Punch tests and safety

Secure your phone, use the wrist strap, and clear the space around you before a speed or power test. Follow the in-app safety guidance. Scores and sport-fit suggestions are estimates for fun and comparison. They are not medical, coaching, scouting, or laboratory measurements.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases on the Premium screen with the same Apple Account that made the purchase. To manage or cancel your subscription, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Leaderboards

Leaderboards use a generated player name and ID by default; signing in with Apple is optional and lets you choose your name. To delete your account and scores, go to Profile > Leaderboard Account > Delete account. To report an inappropriate name, press and hold it on the leaderboard and tap Report, or email us with the name and score.

Privacy requests

Use the same support address for privacy or data deletion requests and include the app name. See the Privacy Policy.
