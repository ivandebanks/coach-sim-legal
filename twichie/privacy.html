Twichie Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026 · Applies to the Twichie iPhone app.

Twichie measures your reaction time, estimates punch speed with your iPhone's motion sensors, and shows a Power Score. No account is required. This policy explains what stays on your device and what is sent off it.

Data that stays on your device

Your profile (body weight, height, fist size, glove weight, stance, age, sex, and boxing experience), your test history, personal bests, settings, and the raw motion-sensor readings used during a test are stored on your iPhone only. We do not receive them. You can remove them by deleting the app.

Motion sensors

Twichie reads your iPhone's motion sensors only while you run a punch-speed or Power Score test, to estimate hand speed and acceleration. Readings are processed on your device.

Online leaderboards and optional Apple sign-in

You can use Twichie without an account. By default the leaderboards use a random player ID created on your device and a generated display name such as "Player 1A2B". Optionally, you can sign in with Apple in Settings to choose the name other players see. We request no name or email from Apple; the app keeps only Apple's opaque user identifier on your device, and only a one-way hash of it is sent to our leaderboard database as your player ID. When you complete a test, the app sends your player ID, display name, the leaderboard (reflex, punch speed, or Power Score), and your score to our leaderboard database, hosted by Supabase. Other players can see your display name and score. Names are filtered for objectionable words, and players can report a name from the leaderboard. The leaderboard does not receive your profile details, raw motion data, or test history.

Purchases and subscriptions

Twichie Premium is an auto-renewing subscription. Apple handles payment, and we do not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user ID and purchase or receipt information to confirm that your subscription is active, unlock Premium, and restore purchases. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Sharing

Free use asks you to share Twichie using the standard iOS share sheet. What you share and who you share it with is handled by iOS and the app you choose. Twichie only learns whether the share sheet was completed.

Information we do not collect

No account, name, email address, or phone number. No camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or location access. No Apple Health data. No advertising SDKs, advertising identifiers, analytics providers, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

Children

Twichie is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Retention, access, and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard is in operation. If you signed in with Apple, you can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile > Leaderboard Account > Delete account, which immediately removes your display name and all of your leaderboard scores from our servers. Tests and profile details stored on your device are not affected and can be removed by deleting the app. You can also revoke Twichie's Apple sign-in access in Settings > Apple Account > Sign-In & Security. Without an account, reinstalling the app creates a new player ID, so older entries stay on the leaderboard unless removed; to ask us to delete them or to ask what we hold, email snapit.foranything@gmail.com with the leaderboard name and scores as they appear in the app. To report an inappropriate name, use the Report option on the leaderboard or email the same address; we review reports promptly and remove offending names. We use what you send only to respond to you.
